# AuraSound Wave Pro Wireless Earbuds

The AuraSound Wave Pro are true wireless earbuds designed for everyday listening and workouts. They use Bluetooth 5.3 for a stable connection and feature active noise cancellation to reduce background hum on commutes and calls.

Each earbud weighs 4.6 grams and the charging case provides up to 28 hours of total playback. Touch controls let you play, pause, and skip without reaching for your phone. The earbuds carry an IPX5 water-resistance rating, so light rain and sweat are not a problem.

A dual-microphone array improves voice clarity on calls. The Wave Pro support multipoint connection, so you can stay linked to your laptop and phone at the same time. They charge over USB-C and a 10-minute quick charge gives about 2 hours of listening.

The product is aimed at commuters, runners, and remote workers who want a comfortable, low-latency audio companion without a premium price.
